Frequently asked questions

What does a bobbin transport system do?

It carries roving bobbins on an overhead track from roving frames to ring frames, so bobbins are not handled, damaged or mixed up.

Can any roving frame feed any ring frame?

Yes. iaBTS-Cruzo lets you group any roving frame with any ring frame, with first-in-first-out delivery.

How does it prevent count mix-up?

Foolproof PLC controls route each bobbin to the right ring frame.
