Why Auto Cone-Pack

Benefits

  • Cone weight control

    Stops weight in excess of the mill's standard going into each box or bag.

  • No mix-ups

    Eliminates errors from cone and yarn mix-ups.

  • Defect stop

    Stops defective cones using UV inspection.

  • Packing report

    End of shift and end of day data, ready for your ERP.

  • Consistent packing

    Every bag and box packed the same way, every shift.

  • Manpower saving

    Packing no longer depends on finding labour in peak season.

How it works

Process

  1. Weight measurement

    Cones are weighed in sets for each bag or box, with smart weight control assistance and net and gross weight logging.

  2. Cone orientation

    Cones are tilted to the right orientation to fit into bags.

  3. Polythene wrapping

    Cones are wrapped and sealed for consistent quality.

  4. Bag or box packing

    Cones are placed in layers according to the configuration chosen.

  5. Taping and strapping

    Boxes are taped, strapped and gross-weighed.

  6. Labelling and packing data

    A barcode label is printed with the packing information. Shift and day data is consolidated and sent to the mill ERP.

Frequently asked questions

Can Auto Cone-Pack pack into both bags and cartons?

Yes. There are box (carton) and bag (sack) models, and several customers run both. Sizes and layer configurations are set to your requirement.

How does it stop weight give-away?

Cones are weighed in sets for each bag or box. Smart weight control keeps each package close to your standard, and net and gross weights are logged.

Does it connect to our ERP?

Yes. A barcode label is printed for every package and shift and day packing data is consolidated for your ERP or mill information system.

Can it inspect cones before packing?

Yes. Add CONE-i to stop cones with the wrong count, colour or UV-visible defects before they are packed.

How much output can it handle?

GHCL's Shree Meenakshi Mills packs 20,000 kg a day with one operator per shift, and Sudiva Spinners packs 36 tonnes a day.
