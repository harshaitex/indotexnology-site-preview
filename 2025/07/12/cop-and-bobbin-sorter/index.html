SortLink Magic: The Smart Revolution in Cop and Bobbin Sorting

SortLink Magic automates cop and bobbin sorting on autowinders with AI vision: up to 4 colours, full and remnant bobbins, no skilled operator.

When innovation meets customer-centric thinking, the result is transformation — and SortLink Magic is a perfect example.

At Indo Texnology, we are proud to have introduced India’s first fully automated copsorter and bobbin sorter for magazine-type winders and autoconers. Designed to address the everyday challenges of textile mills, this pioneering solution changed how spinning mills manage their cop handling and winding section efficiencies.

The Problem With Traditional Bobbin Sorting

Before SortLink, sorting bobbins or cops based on color, fullness, and rejection was a highly manual, error-prone, and labor-intensive process. Textile mills struggled with:

  • High manpower costs
  • Operator fatigue
  • Inconsistent bobbin segregation
  • Lack of real-time data on autoconer performance

Our first-generation SortLink copsorter system addressed many of these issues by automating bobbin segregation using sensors, conveyors, and pneumatic pistons. It enabled color-based and size-based sorting while reducing reliance on manual labor.

But we didn’t stop there.

Listening to Customers: The Key to Innovation

As a fast-growing and customer-focused textile automation company, Indo Texnology believes in continuous improvement. Based on direct feedback from spinning mills, we identified 3 major areas for enhancement in the original system:

  1. Space Constraints – The earlier copsorter required additional floor space, which was difficult to accommodate in many mills.
  2. Sensor Limitations – Traditional sensors struggled to detect close color shades in bobbins or cops.
  3. Maintenance Complexity – Multiple conveyors and pistons meant more components to maintain and higher air consumption.

Enter: SortLink Magic — The Next-Gen Copsorter & Bobbin Sorter

To overcome these challenges, our R&D team, led by Harsha, engineered the SortLink Magic — a smarter, more compact, and powerful AI-based copsorter and bobbin sorter designed for today’s high-efficiency textile mills.

🌟 Key Features of SortLink Magic:

  • ✅ Fits into existing collection tray space – No extra layout changes required
  • ✅ AI + Camera-based vision system – Detects even the closest of shade variations with high precision
  • ✅ One conveyor. One motor. One piston. – Minimal moving parts, reducing maintenance and energy consumption
  • ✅ Remote diagnostics enabled – 24×7 support and troubleshooting
  • ✅ Real-time performance data – View full bobbin, remnant bobbin, and rejection metrics directly

Proven Performance Across Leading Mills

Since its launch, SortLink Magic copsorter systems have seen massive adoption with over 1,500 units installed across India and 4+ countries. Leading textile groups trust this innovation to power their productivity:

  • Vardhman Group – 120+ systems installed
  • SCM Spinners
  • RSWM
  • Ramco Group
  • KG Group
  • SSM Group
    …and many more.

These mills rely on SortLink Magic to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and gain full visibility over their autoconer and winding section performance.

Why Choose Indo Texnology’s Copsorter & Bobbin Sorter?

Indo Texnology stands at the forefront of textile automation in India. With a commitment to continuous innovation, fast response, and customer satisfaction, we offer:

Looking Ahead

SortLink Magic is more than just a machine. It represents a mindset shift in textile manufacturing — where automation meets intelligence and efficiency becomes effortless.
