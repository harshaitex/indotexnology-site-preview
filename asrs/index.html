Automatic storage and retrieval (ASRS) for textiles

Automated storage for yarn, fabric rolls and trims, in association with Craftsman Storage Systems. High-density racking, cranes and shuttles controlled by warehouse management software and synchronised with your ERP.

Applications

ASRS solutions

Fabric roll storage

  • Wide range of fabric rolls, with and without pallets
  • Single-roll picking, eliminating labour
  • High-bay cranes up to 35 m (115 ft) for maximum space saving
  • Warehouse management software (WMS)

Yarn storage

  • Multi-deep shuttle and crane system for high-density yarn storage
  • First-in, first-out (FIFO) and batch control
  • ERP synchronisation
  • Suits low to medium warehouse heights

Trims and sample fabric rolls

  • Vertical storage on the shop floor for sewing trims, packaging trims, labels and small parts
  • User-access controlled inventory with ERP sync
  • Heights from 3 m to 24 m

Custom material handling

  • Hoists and lifts
  • Overhead conveyor transfer systems for pallets and fabric rolls

Frequently asked questions

What can be stored in a textile ASRS?

Yarn packages, fabric rolls (with or without pallets), sewing and packaging trims, labels and sample fabric rolls.

How tall can the storage go?

High-bay cranes reach up to 35 m (115 ft). Vertical trim storage ranges from 3 m to 24 m.

Does it work with our ERP?

Yes. Warehouse management software controls FIFO and batches and synchronises with your ERP.
