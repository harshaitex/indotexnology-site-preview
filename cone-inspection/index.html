CONE-i: computer vision cone inspection

Automatic cone inspection with AI vision. CONE-i checks every cone before packing, catches count and colour mix-ups and UV-visible contamination, and stops the line straight away. Works as an add-on to Auto Cone-Pack or as a standalone station.

Running with Auto Cone-Pack at Sudiva Spinners: 36 tonnes a day with zero packing defects.

CONE-i capabilities

Cone tip identification

  • Alerts and stops the line instantly
  • Prevents count mix-ups reaching the buyer

UV defect analysis

  • Defective cones are stopped before packing

Frequently asked questions

Can CONE-i work without Auto Cone-Pack?

Yes. It is available as an add-on to Auto Cone-Pack and as a standalone inspection station.

What does CONE-i detect?

Variations in cone tip design or colour (count and article mix-ups) and UV-visible defects such as contamination.
