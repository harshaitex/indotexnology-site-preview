About us

Indo Texnology Private Limited is a Coimbatore-based engineering company specialising in automation and inspection systems for textile manufacturing. We design, manufacture and commission complete material-handling and quality-inspection lines for spinning mills: bobbin transport between roving and ring frames, cone transport from winding to packing, AI-based cone inspection, automatic weighing and packing, and automated storage.

Mr. S. Thirupathi is the conceptualiser of multiple ideas for the Indian market, Indian customers and workmanship. With 40 years of expertise in the textile industry, he has a deep commitment to customers. He has travelled to most textile-producing countries and has a comprehensive understanding of the process.

Delivering innovation

Our DNA

We look for problems which do not have solutions yet. We conceive the cost-effective solution, evaluate it, and then commercialise it. Products we have introduced to the market include automatic waste baling, automatic cone packing with inspection, and bobbin sorting.

  1. Listening to the customer

    We pay attention to our customers and work out their real pain points and concerns.

  2. Identifying the problem

    We find and understand problems that have no answer yet, and decide what can be done.

  3. Conceptualising the solution

    Once the scope is clear, we design a cost-effective solution that meets every requirement of the user.

  4. Implementation

    We build it, run it in a working mill and check it performs as intended before we sell it.

  5. Enlarging the scope

    We keep going after the first fix, extending the solution to meet future demands and challenges.
